HOW TO PLAY

THE WAR

Dominion bombers run for the drop corridor under BASTION. Every one that dives through strikes MILLER's planetary shield, AEGIS. Their fighters escort them and hunt your guns; capital ships stand off and shell the station.

Lose AEGIS or lose BASTION and the war is over. Hold for twenty waves.

BUILD

Pick a platform (1–4), then a glowing slot. Select a platform to refit it to Mk II and III, or sell it. Select BASTION to upgrade the station itself.
