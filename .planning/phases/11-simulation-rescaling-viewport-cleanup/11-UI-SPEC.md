## Enemy LOD Visual Contract (REND-04)

### LOD Tiers

| Tier | Distance | Rendering | Visual |
|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| Full geometry | < 10,000 km | Instanced mesh (existing) | 3D enemy model with archetype color |
| Billboard | 10,000 – 50,000 km | GL_POINTS via trajPg shader | Colored dot, sized by archetype |
| Skip | > 50,000 km | Not rendered | Invisible |

### LOD Transition

- **Hard cut** — no cross-fade, no alpha blending (user decision)
- Per-type grouping: billboard enemies rendered in archetype batches (same shader, different color/size uniforms per group)
- Depth testing enabled during billboard rendering (gl.DEPTH_TEST active, gl_FragDepthEXT for log depth)

### Billboard Dot Sizing

Proportional to archetype threat/size. Capital ships (8.0px) are clearly distinguished from Grunts (3.0px) at a glance.

Source: CONTEXT.md (three LOD levels, hard cut, billboard sized by archetype), RESEARCH.md (LOD thresholds, billboard approach)

## BH Close-up Visual Contract (REND-01)

### Distance-Adaptive Parameters

| Parameter | Far (> 30 units) | Close (< 5 units) | Interpolation |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| FBM octave count | 2 (early break) | 4 (full octaves) | closeupFactor gate |
| Photon ring width | 2.0 | 3.5 (sharper) | mix(2.0, 3.5, closeupFactor) |
| Post-loop glow | 1.0x | 1.5x | 1.0 + closeupFactor * 0.5 |
| Disk noise warp | Current | Slightly increased | Proportional to closeupFactor |

### Constraints

- `closeupFactor = smoothstep(30.0, 5.0, u_camDist)` — zero at far distances, one when close
- Must maintain 30fps floor with enemies on screen
- Must not regress BH appearance at far distances (closeupFactor = 0 at far range = unchanged)
- All changes gated behind distance — zero performance cost at far distances
- Must pass existing tests.html shader invariant checks

Source: CONTEXT.md (distance-adaptive, 30fps floor, no far-distance regression), RESEARCH.md (closeupFactor mechanism, parameter recommendations)
