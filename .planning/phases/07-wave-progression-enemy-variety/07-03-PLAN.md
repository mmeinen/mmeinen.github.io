- Keep heading aimed at player for firing orientation

**Sniper AI_DISENGAGE:**
- When `enemies.type[i] === ETYPE.SNIPER`: stronger retrograde (0.5x velocity). Transition to REORBIT at dist > 35 (further than Grunt's 20).

**combat.js changes -- Capital AI:**

3. Add Capital-specific behavior in `updateEnemyAI()`. **All Capital thresholds MUST come from `getArchetypeStats(ETYPE.CAPITAL, waveNumber)`** via the `stats` variable.

**IMPORTANT -- Capital auxTimer lifecycle:**
Capital uses `enemies.auxTimer[i]` for TWO sequential purposes:
- **Phase 1 (warp-in):** auxTimer starts at -1.0 (set by spawnWave in waves.js). auxTimer increments by simDt in EVERY AI state (IDLE, ALERT, TRANSFER, ATTACK). While auxTimer < 0, the Capital is in warp-in phase and the scale ramp is active in updateInstanceBuffer. The Capital should NOT fire or spawn minions during warp-in.
- **Phase 2 (normal operation):** Once auxTimer reaches 0.0, it becomes the minion spawn timer. It continues incrementing by simDt. When it reaches stats.attackCooldown (~10.0s), minions spawn and it resets to 0.

Therefore: auxTimer MUST be incremented by simDt at the TOP of the Capital AI branch, BEFORE any state-specific logic, so it increments regardless of which AI state the Capital is in. Gate all Capital combat actions (firing, minion spawning) on `auxTimer >= 0`.

**Capital AI_IDLE:**
- Use `stats.detectRadius` for detection. Increment auxTimer by simDt (warp-in phase or spawn timer).

**Capital AI_ALERT:**
- Longer pause: 1.0s. Capitals are slow to react. Increment auxTimer by simDt.
- When alert pause expires, set `enemies.aiState[i] = AI_ATTACK` directly (skip AI_TRANSFER -- Capitals stay at station).

**Capital AI_TRANSFER:**
- If a Capital somehow enters TRANSFER, immediately set state to AI_ATTACK. Capitals do NOT transfer. Increment auxTimer by simDt.

**Capital AI_ATTACK:**
- When `enemies.type[i] === ETYPE.CAPITAL`:
- Increment auxTimer by simDt (this handles both warp-in countdown and spawn timer).
- If auxTimer < 0: Capital is still warping in. Do station-keeping only. Skip all combat actions.
- If auxTimer >= 0: Capital is fully materialized.
  - Station-keep: maintain position near assigned body (same logic as AI_IDLE station-keeping but with heading aimed at player).
  - **Minion spawning:** When `auxTimer >= stats.attackCooldown` (expected ~10.0s):
    - Spawn 2-3 Grunt minions (`2 + Math.floor(Math.random() * 2)`)
    - For each minion: check `freeSlots.length > 0` before spawning
    - Spawn position: offset from Capital position by random angle, radius 3.0 units
    - Call `spawnEnemy(mx, 0, mz, ETYPE.GRUNT, -1, 0)` -- minions have no assigned body (-1), they go straight to hunting
    - Set minion AI state directly to AI_ALERT with aiTimer = 0.5 (skip idle, immediately engage)
    - Reset auxTimer to 0
    - Spawn small muzzle flash at Capital position for visual hangar bay launch effect
  - **Heavy shots:** Every 5 seconds (use a separate counter -- add `enemies.hasFired[i]` as a second timer by toggling it, OR more cleanly: fire when `auxTimer` crosses 5.0 on its way to 10.0, i.e. `if (oldAuxTimer < 5.0 && auxTimer >= 5.0)`). Call `enemyFireAt(i, playerPos, playerVel)` for a single kinetic round.
- Capital does NOT disengage or reorbit. It stays in AI_ATTACK until killed.
